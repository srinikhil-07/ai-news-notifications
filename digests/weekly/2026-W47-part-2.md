- [Last year we built megakernels. This year we deleted them: The road to Together Compiler](https://www.together.ai/blog/road-to-together-compiler) — 2026-11-16
  **AI summary (partial article):** Megakernel\-based approaches are not universally superior; simple, flexible use of existing primitives with proper benchmarking can achieve most gains, and rigidity of megakernels limits applicability across hardware and models\.
  - Torch Compile and Inductor heuristics can mislead performance due to limited benchmarking realism and complex flag interactions\.
  - Using CUDA Graphs and Programmatic Dependent Launch with standard kernels recovers most megakernel gains \(about 98%\), exposing megakernel rigidity and suggesting a move toward flexible optimization strategies\.
